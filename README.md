☀️ SunDamage Awareness – ElleHacks Project
Welcome to our ElleHacks project! We created an informative and interactive website to spread awareness about sun damage and sunburn prevention. This project highlights the importance of protecting your skin and staying safe in the sun, using a clean and accessible front-end design built with HTML and CSS.

💡 What This Project Does
1. Educates users about the causes and effects of sun damage and sunburn
2. Provides tips and preventive measures to protect your skin
3. Shares general information on UV radiation and its long-term impacts
4. Features a user-friendly, visually appealing layout to engage viewers

🔧 Built With
- HTML
- CSS
- Javascript

📽️ Presentation
As part of our ElleHacks submission, we also created a presentation that summarizes our research and design process. The presentation includes:

- Background on sun damage and why it matters
- Key facts and statistics about sunburn and skin health
- Overview of our website features and design goals
- Our team's motivation and future directions

👩‍💻 Team & Hackathon
This project was created during ElleHacks, a hackathon that empowers women and non-binary individuals in tech. It was an opportunity to combine creativity, technical skills, and health advocacy in a meaningful way.
